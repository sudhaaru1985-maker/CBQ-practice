Quick Notes Iron Deficiency Anaemia (IDA)
Low Hgb · Low MCV (microcytic) · Low ferritin (<30 ng/mL) · Low serum iron · High TIBC
In an older man, IDA = hidden bleeding until proven otherwise. The GI tract (colon cancer, ulcer) is the most common source. Colonoscopy rules this out. Direct Coombs test is for haemolytic anaemia — NOT needed here.
Growing baby demands more iron. Mother's iron stores deplete, especially in 3rd trimester. Give oral iron supplementation. Check serum ferritin to confirm.
🤰 Pregnant woman + Low Hgb + Low MCV → Iron deficiency first!

Sickle Cell Sickle Cell Anaemia — Pain Crisis
Sickle-shaped RBCs block small blood vessels → Ischemia (lack of oxygen to tissues). This causes painful vaso-occlusive crises affecting bones, chest, and abdomen. NOT coagulopathy, NOT vasculitis.
Sickle/crescent-shaped RBCs · Target cells · Normocytic anaemia · Howell-Jolly bodies (functional asplenia)
⚠️ Mechanism of pain = ISCHEMIA (vaso-occlusion by sickle cells)
Special Dark Urine at Night — What is this?

Patient passes dark urine at night & in the morning, but normal urine during the day. This is Paroxysmal Nocturnal Haemoglobinuria (PNH).

During sleep, CO₂ accumulates → blood pH drops → activates complement → destroys RBCs inside vessels (intravascular haemolysis) → free haemoglobin spills into urine (haemoglobinuria). Answer = C: Premature destruction of RBCs within blood vessels.

Key Clue Von Willebrand Disease

vWD = most common inherited bleeding disorder. vWF acts as a bridge for platelets AND carries Factor VIII.

🔑 Ristocetin test → decreased aggregation = vWD. Both aPTT AND bleeding time are prolonged.
Haemophilia A: Bleeding time NORMAL (platelets fine). Only aPTT prolonged.
vWD: Bleeding time PROLONGED (platelet adhesion affected) + aPTT prolonged.
Also, vWD affects females too — haemophilia A/B mainly affects males (X-linked).
DIC Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation
Sepsis (most common) · Placental abruption · Snake bites · Burns · Trauma · Graft vs host disease
↓ Platelets · ↑ PT · ↑ aPTT · ↑ Thrombin time · ↓ Fibrinogen · ↑↑ D-dimer · Schistocytes on smear
⚠️ Everything is abnormal in DIC — clotting factors AND platelets are consumed!
Thrombocytopenia Low Platelet Count — How to Approach
1. Is there an obvious cause? (drug, infection, DIC) → yes → treat cause
2. No obvious cause → do bone marrow biopsy
3. Bone marrow shows no megakaryocytes → aplastic anaemia or infiltrative disease
4. Bone marrow shows normal/↑ megakaryocytes → peripheral destruction → likely ITP
5. Confirm ITP with serological studies (anti-platelet antibodies)
ITP = Immune Thrombocytopenic Purpura — body makes antibodies that destroy platelets. No splenomegaly.

🟡 Leukaemia

Concept What is Leukaemia?

Leukaemia = cancer of white blood cells. Abnormal immature WBCs (blasts) crowd out normal cells in the bone marrow → anaemia + thrombocytopenia + infections.

Case Focus 10-yr-old girl with gum bleeding & fever

Hgb 5.3 · WBC 60,000 · 60% Promyelocytes + 33% Myeloblasts · Platelets 5000 · D-dimer elevated

Diagnosis: AML-M3 (Acute Promyelocytic Leukaemia)
The leukemic promyelocytes release tissue factor → activates coagulation cascade systemically → DIC.
This causes: Bleeding (low platelets + consumed factors) + bruising + prolonged PT/aPTT + elevated D-dimer.
⚠️ AML-M3 + DIC = EMERGENCY. Treat with All-trans retinoic acid (ATRA).

Quick Notes NHL Subtypes — Key Features
Low grade · Indolent (slow growing) · t(14;18) translocation → overexpresses BCL-2 (anti-apoptosis protein) → cells don't die · Difficult to cure but manageable
Most common NHL (~30-40%) · Intermediate grade · Aggressive but potentially curable with chemotherapy (R-CHOP) · Rapidly enlarging lymph node mass
Low grade B-cell lymphoma · Common sites: stomach (H. pylori!), lung, thyroid, salivary gland · Gastric MALT can regress with H. pylori eradication
High grade B-cell lymphoma · t(8;14) — MYC oncogene overexpression · 100% proliferation index (Ki-67 = 100%) · "Starry sky" pattern on histology · Endemic in Africa, associated with EBV
Intermediate to aggressive · t(11;14) → Cyclin D1 overexpression · Arises from mantle zone of lymphoid follicles · May have widespread disease (nodes, spleen, GI)
